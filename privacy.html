Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Social Video Automation uses information when connecting to Google, YouTube, and TikTok services.

Purpose of the Application

Social Video Automation is a private automation tool used to create, manage, upload, schedule, and publish short-form video content to authorized social media accounts.

Google and YouTube Data

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to obtain authorization from the account owner.

The application may access YouTube account information and permissions necessary to upload, schedule, and manage YouTube content.

TikTok Data

The application uses TikTok Login Kit to allow an account owner to authorize access.

The application may access basic authorized TikTok profile information and permissions required to upload or publish video content through TikTok's Content Posting API.

How Data Is Used

Google, YouTube, and TikTok authorization data is used only to perform operations requested by the account owner, including uploading, scheduling, managing, and publishing video content.

Data Storage

OAuth authorization credentials and access tokens may be stored securely for the purpose of maintaining authorized access and refreshing access when required.

The application does not request or store the user's Google, YouTube, or TikTok password.

Data Sharing

User data obtained through Google, YouTube, or TikTok is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke Google or YouTube access through their Google Account security settings. Users can revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings or connected app controls.
