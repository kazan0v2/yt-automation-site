YouTube Automation

YouTube Automation is a private content automation tool used to manage, upload, and schedule video content on YouTube.

Purpose of the application

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to securely access the authorized YouTube account and perform requested YouTube content management operations.

Google and YouTube Access

Access to Google and YouTube services is granted only after the account owner completes Google's OAuth authorization process.

The application does not request or use Google account credentials such as passwords.
