Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Automation uses information when connecting to Google and YouTube services.

Purpose of the Application

YouTube Automation is a private automation tool used to manage, upload, and schedule video content on an authorized YouTube account.

Google User Data

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to obtain authorization from the account owner.

The application may access YouTube account information and permissions necessary to upload, schedule, and manage YouTube content.

How Data Is Used

Google and YouTube authorization data is used only to perform operations requested by the account owner, including uploading, scheduling, and managing YouTube content.

Data Storage

OAuth authorization credentials may be stored securely for the purpose of maintaining authorized access to the YouTube account and refreshing access when required.

The application does not request or store the user's Google password.

Data Sharing

Google user data obtained by this application is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
